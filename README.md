# Cyber Threat Intelligence Reports

A collection of independent Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) assessments focused on threat actor activity, cyber espionage, attack techniques, infrastructure, and attribution.

These reports emphasize threats targeting the Defense Industrial Base (DIB) and apply structured intelligence analysis, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, and publicly available threat intelligence.

## Research Methodology

My assessments follow a structured CTI research process:

1. Threat actor profiling and historical activity review
2. Campaign and targeting analysis
3. MITRE ATT&CK technique mapping
4. Infrastructure and attribution assessment
5. Intelligence documentation using OpenCTI and MISP
6. Analytical confidence evaluation and identification of intelligence gaps

## Tools and Frameworks

- OpenCTI
- MISP
- MITRE ATT&CK
- Open-source intelligence (OSINT)
- Structured analytic techniques
